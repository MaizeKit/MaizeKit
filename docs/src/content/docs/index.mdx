## How it works

Most corn mazes post a map on a sign at the entrance. MaizeKit lines a photo of that map up with
the field on satellite imagery and turns it into a page that shows your GPS position on the maze
and the way out.

## What visitors get

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="You are here" icon="seti:pipeline">A blue dot on the maze map, from the phone's GPS.</Card>
  <Card title="The way out" icon="right-arrow">A route along the real paths to the exit, from wherever you're standing.</Card>
  <Card title="Fix my spot" icon="pencil">GPS a row off? Tap where you really are and the map lines up.</Card>
  <Card title="Private" icon="approve-check">No server and no accounts. Location never leaves the phone.</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Add a maze

Each maze is one folder in the [MaizeKit repository](https://github.com/MaizeKit/MaizeKit): a photo of
the maze map and a `maze.config.json`. It gets a page at `maizekit.github.io/m/your-farm/` and a
printable entrance sign with a QR code. Run a maze?
[Send us your map](https://github.com/MaizeKit/MaizeKit/issues/new?template=add-maze.yml). It's free.
