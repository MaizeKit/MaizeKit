MaizeKit

Turn a photo of a corn maze map into a phone app that shows where you are and the way out.

Use the template See a live maze

Free and open source. Static files on GitHub Pages. No server, no accounts.

  • You are here

    A blue dot on the maze map, from the phone's GPS.

  • The way out, from anywhere

    A route along the maze's real paths to the exit, with distance and steps.

  • Fix my spot

    GPS off by a row? Tap where you really are and the map lines up.

  • Steady in the corn

    The route holds through GPS jitter instead of jumping between rows.

  • Private by design

    Location stays on the phone. Nothing is sent or stored anywhere.

  • Works on weak signal

    The app and the map tiles you've seen are saved on the phone.

How it works

  1. 1

    Photograph the map

    A straight-on photo of the maze sign at the entrance.

  2. 2

    Describe it

    In maze.config.json: the field outline, the entrance and exit, and four corners on the ground.

  3. 3

    Build

    python tools/build_assets.py finds the paths and lines the map up with the real field.

  4. 4

    Publish

    Push to GitHub and turn on Pages. Share the link at the entrance.
