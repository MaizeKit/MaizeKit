Turn a photo of a corn maze map into a phone app that shows where you are and the way out.

## Overview

MaizeKit is a template for a small web app (a PWA) for one corn maze. Visitors open
it on their phone, allow location, and see a blue dot on the maze map with a line
to the exit from wherever they're standing.

It's static files only: no server, no accounts, no build step for the app itself.
Location is used on the phone and never sent anywhere.
